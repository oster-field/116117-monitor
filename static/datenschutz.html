Terminüberwachung (E-Mail-Adresse, Vermittlungscode, PLZ)

  • Zweck: Prüfen freier Termine im Umkreis von 150 km um Ihre PLZ und Benachrichtigung per E-Mail.
  • Rechtsgrundlage: Ihre ausdrückliche Einwilligung, Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a und Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a DSGVO. Da der Dienst auf Psychiatrie ausgerichtet ist und der Vermittlungscode von einer Überweisung stammt, können die Angaben Rückschlüsse auf Ihre Gesundheit zulassen. Die Einwilligung erteilen Sie per Checkbox vor dem Start.
  • Widerruf: jederzeit mit Wirkung für die Zukunft, über „Überwachung stoppen“ (der Eintrag wird sofort gelöscht) oder per E-Mail. Die bisherige Verarbeitung bleibt rechtmäßig.
  • Speicherung: Die E-Mail-Adresse wird verschlüsselt in einer Datenbank auf dem Server gespeichert. Ebenfalls gespeichert werden Vermittlungscode, PLZ, Status und Zeitpunkt der letzten Prüfung.
  • Dauer: bis Sie die Überwachung stoppen oder die Löschung verlangen. Nach einem gemeldeten Termin endet die Überwachung; der Eintrag bleibt bis zu Ihrer Löschungsanfrage gespeichert.

What this is about

Termin-Wächter automatically checks whether the 116117 Terminservice has free appointments for psychiatry and neurology and notifies you by email. This policy describes which data is processed for that.

Appointment monitoring (email address, referral code, postal code)

  • Purpose: checking for free appointments within 150 km of your postal code and notifying you by email.
  • Legal basis: your explicit consent, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. Because the service targets psychiatry and the referral code comes from a referral slip, the data can reveal information about your health. You give consent with a checkbox before starting.
  • Withdrawal: at any time with effect for the future, via “Stop Monitoring” (your entry is deleted immediately) or by email. Processing carried out before remains lawful.
  • Storage: your email address is stored encrypted in a database on the server. The referral code, postal code, status and time of the last check are stored as well.
  • Duration: until you stop the monitoring or request deletion. After an appointment has been reported, monitoring ends; the entry stays stored until you request its deletion.
  • Obligation to provide data: providing the data is voluntary, but the service cannot be used without it.

Recipients

  • 116117 Terminservice (KBV): to check availability, the server queries www.116117-termine.de on your behalf every 10 minutes using your referral code and postal code. This data therefore reaches the operator of that site, whose own privacy policy applies.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc., USA): email delivery, processor under Art. 28 GDPR. Your email address and the message content are transmitted. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Resend is certified) and standard contractual clauses.
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (Oracle): server hosting, processor under Art. 28 GDPR.
  • Operator: for operational monitoring, the operator receives an internal email for every new request (email address, referral code, postal code), a copy of every appointment notification and an alert when checks keep failing for a longer time.

Server log files

When you open the site, the web server technically processes your IP address, date and time, the requested address, the status code and browser information (user agent). The purpose is secure and stable operation; the legal basis is legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Log files are deleted after 14 days.

Local storage in your browser

While monitoring is running, your browser keeps an entry for it (tw_job: request ID, email address, booking link) so the status reappears after a reload. It is necessary for the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and is removed when you stop or when an appointment is found. Your cookie choice is stored as tw_consent. No first-party cookies are set.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is only loaded with your consent (banner on the start page). Without consent, no connection to Google is made.

With consent, Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; parent company Google LLC, USA) sets cookies and transmits usage data, such as page views, approximate location, device and browser details and a pseudonymous identifier, to measure how the site is used. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Google LLC is certified).
